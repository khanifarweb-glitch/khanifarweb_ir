## Who is it for?

If you run a business and don't have a website yet, or your current site can't win you customers, website design starts here: a site that knows who its audience is and what it should ask them to do.

## Types of websites

- **Personal website:** introduces your expertise, track record and ways to reach you.
- **Service website:** presents your services, shows your work and collects customer requests.
- **Online store:** displays products and makes the path to purchase simple.

## What every project includes

- Page structure based on your business goals, not on taste
- Correct display on mobile, tablet and desktop
- Good loading speed
- Basic SEO: titles, descriptions, heading structure, sitemap
- Basic accessibility: contrast, keyboard focus and image alt text
- Support and further development after delivery
