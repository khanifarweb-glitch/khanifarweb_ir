## Which problems do I solve?

- Your site is slow or doesn't display well on mobile
- Your site has become outdated and needs updating
- You have an error or technical problem and nobody is responding
- You want a new feature or page added
- You don't have a dedicated person looking after your site

## What gets done?

- Fixing errors and technical problems
- Updating the look, content and sections of the site
- Developing new features
- Speed and SEO optimization
- Maintenance and periodic checks

## How do we start?

Write your website address and the problem or change you want. I'll first review the state of the site and then propose a solution.
